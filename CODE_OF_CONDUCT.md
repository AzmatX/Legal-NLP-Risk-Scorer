# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to maintaining a professional, respectful, and collaborative environment for all contributors.

Participation in this project requires treating fellow contributors with respect regardless of experience level, background, or technical expertise.

---

## Expected Behavior

Contributors are expected to:

* Communicate respectfully and professionally
* Accept constructive feedback
* Focus discussions on technical improvement
* Support collaborative problem solving
* Respect differing viewpoints and approaches
* Maintain academic and professional integrity

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not acceptable:

* Harassment or discrimination
* Personal attacks
* Offensive or abusive language
* Deliberate disruption of project activities
* Misrepresentation of another contributor's work
* Any form of unethical academic conduct

---

## Professional Standards

This repository values:

* Transparency
* Technical excellence
* Reproducibility
* Documentation
* Responsible AI development

---

## Enforcement

Project maintainers reserve the right to remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior violates this Code of Conduct.

---

## Acknowledgement

This Code of Conduct exists to promote productive collaboration and maintain a healthy engineering and research environment.
